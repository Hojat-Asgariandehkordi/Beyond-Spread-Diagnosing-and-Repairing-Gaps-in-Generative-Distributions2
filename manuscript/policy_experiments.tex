\subsection{Frozen policies expose the quality--coverage--cost operating point}
\label{sec:policy}
The original CIFAR selector attains mean KID 0.0790 across two/four/eight-call
budgets, versus 0.1039 for leading DDIM and 0.0784 for both Vendi and
teacher-feature Fr\'echet selection. The selector chose full-moment initialization at two calls. The later
centered-covariance confirmation tests a particular intervention separately
from the comparison of selection rules.

For text-to-image, the rule screens two fast samplers on eight diagnosis
seeds per prompt. It requires preference of at least 0.475, CLIP alignment loss
at most 0.01, DINO and CLIP spread ratios at least 0.9, DINO coverage at least 0.8,
and both support-tail excesses at most 0.05. It picks the highest-coverage
eligible sampler or uses the 16-call SD or 20-call Sana fallback. Sixteen
independent seeds per prompt evaluate each frozen choice. The six outcome
gates use one-sided 95\% prompt-bootstrap intervals for preference, alignment,
spread, and support, with preference floor 0.45. Coverage is reported
separately. Both audits pass, as do quality plus Vendi and fixed Sana
DPM++8 (Supp.~Secs.~J--K).
